# HTML/CSS Color Guide

<Goals>

- Professional, accessible color palettes following WCAG standards
- 60-30-10 rule for color balance
- High contrast ratios for readability

</Goals>

## 60-30-10 Rule

- **Primary (60%)**: Cool or light color (backgrounds, large areas)
- **Secondary (30%)**: Cool or light color (cards, sections)
- **Accent (10%)**: Complementary hot color (CTAs, highlights)

<Standards>

**Backgrounds**: White, off-white, light cool colors (blues, greens), subtle neutral tones. Never purple, magenta, red, orange, yellow, pink.

**Text**: Dark neutrals (#1f2328, #24292f) on light backgrounds. Near-white (#f0f2f3) on dark backgrounds. Never yellow or pink text.

**Hot Colors** (red, orange, yellow): Reserve for alerts, warnings, errors only. Limit to small accent areas.

**Gradients**: Minimal color shifts within same family (e.g., #E6F2FF to #F5F7FA). Linear over radial for backgrounds. Never mix hot and cool in one gradient.

</Standards>

<Limitations>

- No bright purples, magentas, neon colors in general UI
- No low contrast ratios (must pass WCAG)
- No highly saturated hot colors for large areas
- No yellow text (poor readability)

</Limitations>
